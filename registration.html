Registration

At least one author per paper must complete registration (including payment) by May 20, 2026 (Beijing Time, UTC+8) and attend the conference to present the paper.

All presenters and attendees must register online.

Important Notes:

  • The conference registration fee includes: Final paper upload, access to all technical sessions, workshops, and tutorials, a copy of the conference proceedings, coffee breaks, lunches, welcome reception, conference banquet, and farewell party.
  • One regular registration can cover a maximum of 1 paper for upload and presentation.
  • If the maximum paper limit is exceeded, an additional registration is required.

Additional Charges:

  • Extra Paper Charge: An additional fee of 2100 RMB must be paid for each additional paper beyond the first one covered by a single registration, provided the additional paper(s) have no other registered authors.
  • Extra Page Charge: A charge of 1200 RMB per extra page will be required for papers exceeding the standard 6-page limit.

Refund Policy:

  • 50% of the paid registration fee (after deduction of any applicable transaction fees) will be refunded for cancellations made on or before May 21, 2026.
