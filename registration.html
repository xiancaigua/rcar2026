Important Notes

  • Each accepted paper must be covered by at least one valid author registration.
  • One regular registration can cover one paper for upload and presentation.
  • If an author needs to cover additional papers without another registered author, an extra paper charge applies.
  • Registration includes access to technical sessions, workshops/tutorials, conference materials, coffee breaks, lunches, welcome reception, conference banquet, and farewell event, subject to the final conference arrangement.

Additional Charges

  • Extra Paper Charge: 2100 RMB for each additional paper beyond the first one covered by a single registration, if the additional paper has no other registered author.
  • Extra Page Charge: 1200 RMB per extra page for papers exceeding the standard 6-page limit. A maximum of 2 extra pages is allowed.

Refund Policy

Cancellations made on or before June 20, 2026, may receive a 50% refund of the paid registration fee after deduction of applicable transaction fees.

Registration system and payment instructions will be announced soon.
