Call for Abstracts / Extended Abstracts

IEEE RCAR 2026 is opening an additional call for short paper / extended abstract submissions. This call is intended for participants who would like to present recent work, preliminary results, student projects, or emerging research ideas at the conference.

Item Date / Information
Abstract Submission Deadline June 30, 2026
Abstract Acceptance Notification July 10, 2026
  • Accepted abstracts / extended abstracts are expected to be presented at the conference.
  • Short paper / extended abstract submissions will not be submitted to IEEE Xplore and will not be considered archival IEEE publications, unless otherwise announced by the organizing committee.
  • Registration fee standards are based on participant category, not submission type. There is currently no separate discounted registration category for abstract-only presentations unless otherwise announced.
  • Submission instructions and detailed presentation arrangements will be updated after confirmation by the organizing committee.

Full Paper

  • Initial submission length: 4-6 pages.
  • Final submission length: 6 pages.
  • Up to 2 additional pages are allowed with extra page charges.
  • Paper size: A4.
  • Format: IEEE two-column conference format.
  • File type: PDF.
  • Suggested PDF size limit: 5 MB.
  • Accepted and presented full papers are expected to be submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore, subject to IEEE requirements.

Short Paper / Extended Abstract

  • Length: 1-2 pages.
  • Intended for late-breaking results, preliminary research ideas, or extended abstracts.
  • Accepted short papers / extended abstracts may be presented as posters.
  • They will NOT be submitted to IEEE Xplore.
  • They are NOT considered archival IEEE publications.
  • IEEE copyright transfer and IEEE PDF eXpress compliance are not required for short papers / extended abstracts unless the conference committee later specifies otherwise.

Supplementary Material

  • Authors may upload supplementary materials such as videos to demonstrate experimental results.
  • Supplementary materials are optional unless otherwise specified.

Templates

  • LaTeX template: http://ras.papercept.net/conferences/support/tex.php
  • Word template: http://ras.papercept.net/conferences/support/word.php
