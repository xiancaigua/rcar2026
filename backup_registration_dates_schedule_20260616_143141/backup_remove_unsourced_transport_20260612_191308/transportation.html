Transportation

Participants are encouraged to arrange international flights, accommodation, entry procedures, and local transportation early. Seychelles is a popular travel destination, and flight and hotel availability may be limited during the conference period. Conference-specific transportation arrangements will be updated after confirmation by the organizing committee.

International Gateway Seychelles International Airport (SEZ), Mahé Island
Entry Procedure Travel Authorisation is required before departure
Airport Transfer Taxi, public bus, car rental, hotel transfer, or private transfer

Arrival by Air

Most international participants should plan to arrive through Seychelles International Airport (SEZ), located on Mahé Island. Participants should check airline schedules, transit rules, baggage policies, and minimum connection times before booking flights.

  • Recommended arrival airport: Seychelles International Airport (SEZ), Mahé Island.
  • Participants are advised to arrive at least one day before their scheduled presentation or registration activity.
  • Recommended routes and airlines will not be listed as official conference recommendations unless confirmed by the organizing committee.
  • Transit visa requirements may depend on the participant's nationality, airline, and transit country.

Entry Requirements

Seychelles is generally a visa-free destination for visitors, but travelers must still complete the required immigration procedures before traveling. Participants should check the latest official entry requirements before departure.

Item Current Guidance
Visa No visa is generally required for visitors to Seychelles, but a visitor's permit is issued on arrival to eligible travelers.
Travel Authorisation All travelers are required to apply for Travel Authorisation before traveling to Seychelles.
Documents Prepare a valid passport, flight booking confirmation, accommodation confirmation, contact information, trip information, and payment card for the online procedure.
Health-related document A Yellow Fever vaccination certificate may be required only under specific travel or transit conditions involving Yellow Fever endemic countries.
Entry and transit requirements may change. Participants are responsible for checking official sources before departure. The conference website provides travel guidance only and does not replace official immigration advice.

Registered participants who require an invitation letter may contact the conference secretariat at ieeercar@163.com. Please include your paper ID, paper title, full name, affiliation, passport name if needed, and registration confirmation.

Airport Transfer

After arriving at Seychelles International Airport, participants may use taxi services, public buses, car rental, hotel-arranged transfers, or pre-booked private transfers to reach their accommodation or the conference venue.

Option Description Notes
Taxi Taxi services are available at the international airport. Taxi stands are located outside the arrivals terminal. Suitable for late arrivals or participants with luggage.
Public Bus Public buses are accessible outside the airport and connect to different destinations on Mahé. Participants should check bus card and luggage rules before using public buses.
Car Rental Car rental services are available at the airport. Prior booking is recommended. Participants should check local driving rules and parking availability.
Hotel Transfer Some hotels may provide airport transfer services. Availability, price, and booking method depend on the hotel.
Private Transfer Participants may arrange private transfers in advance. Recommended for groups, late arrivals, or participants carrying large luggage.
Public buses may not be convenient for participants with large luggage or late-night arrivals. Participants are encouraged to confirm transportation arrangements before departure.

Local Transportation in Seychelles

Local transportation options in Seychelles include taxis, public buses, rental cars, hotel transfers, and private transfers. The most suitable option depends on the participant's hotel location, luggage, arrival time, and conference schedule.

Option Suitable For Notes
Taxi Short local trips, airport transfer, evening travel Confirm destination and fare arrangement before departure.
Public Bus Budget local travel on Mahé Check routes, operating hours, bus card requirements, and luggage policy.
Rental Car Flexible travel on Mahé or Praslin Useful for independent travel; check driving rules and hotel parking before booking.
Hotel Transfer Airport-hotel or hotel-venue transfer Confirm directly with the hotel in advance.
Private Transfer Groups or participants with large luggage Pre-booking is recommended.

Detailed hotel-to-venue transportation guidance will be updated after the conference venue and recommended accommodation arrangements are finalized.

Inter-island Transportation

Some participants may plan to visit other islands before or after the conference. Inter-island transportation in Seychelles may include domestic flights, chartered air services, and ferry services between islands such as Mahé, Praslin, and La Digue.

  • Inter-island trips are optional and are not included in the conference registration fee unless otherwise specified.
  • Domestic flight and charter options should be checked directly with the relevant operators.
  • Ferry schedules and availability should be checked before planning island visits.
  • Weather and sea conditions may affect ferry schedules.
  • Participants should allow enough time when connecting between ferries, domestic flights, and international flights.

Airport Transportation

The conference hotel and venue is Savoy Seychelles Resort & Spa, located in Beau Vallon, Mahé. Most international participants will arrive at Seychelles International Airport (SEZ). The transfer from the airport to Savoy Seychelles Resort & Spa is approximately 45 minutes by car, depending on traffic and local road conditions.

Taxi Participants may take a taxi from the airport arrival area to Savoy Seychelles Resort & Spa. This is usually the most direct option for individual travelers with luggage.
Hotel-arranged Transfer Participants may contact the hotel or booking channel in advance to ask whether airport transfer service can be arranged. Availability and price depend on the hotel policy.
Pre-booked Private Transfer Private transfer is recommended for groups, late arrivals, or participants carrying large luggage. Please confirm pickup time, meeting point, and fare before arrival.

Useful Travel Tips

  • Book flights and hotels early.
  • Check passport validity and entry requirements before departure.
  • Complete the required Travel Authorisation before departure.
  • Keep accommodation confirmation and return/onward flight information available for entry procedures.
  • Confirm airport transfer before arrival, especially for late-night flights.
  • Check local transportation options before planning independent travel.
  • Allow extra time for airport transfers, ferry connections, and inter-island travel.
  • Keep the conference contact email available: ieeercar@163.com
