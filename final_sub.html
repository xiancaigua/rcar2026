Final Submission

Authors of accepted submissions should prepare their camera-ready files according to the following instructions. Please ensure that the title, abstract, author list, affiliations, and paper metadata in CMT are accurate, as this information will be used for the conference program.

General Requirements

  • Final submission must be completed through CMT.
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the paper at the conference.
  • Final submission deadline: June 20, 2026.
  • Contact: ieee.rcar@gmail.com
Final Submission via CMT

IEEE PDF eXpress Conference ID: To be confirmed

Full Paper Camera-ready Checklist

  1. Revised final manuscript PDF.
  2. IEEE PDF eXpress-compliant PDF.
  3. IEEE electronic copyright receipt.
  4. Similarity report, if required by the conference committee.
  5. One-page digest slide, if required.
  6. Correct paper metadata in CMT.
  7. Registration completed by at least one author.

Short Paper / Extended Abstract Checklist

  1. Revised final short paper / extended abstract PDF.
  2. Correct paper metadata in CMT.
  3. One-page digest slide, if required.
  4. Registration completed by at least one author.
  5. IEEE copyright transfer and IEEE PDF eXpress compliance are not required because short papers / extended abstracts are not submitted to IEEE Xplore.

  • Word template: http://ras.papercept.net/conferences/support/word.php
