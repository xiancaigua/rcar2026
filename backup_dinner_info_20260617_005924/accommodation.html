Hotel Reservation

Notice: August is a peak tourism season in Seychelles. Participants are strongly encouraged to reserve hotel accommodation as early as possible.
Hotel reservations through this arrangement are available until July 20, 2026. The reservation will be confirmed only after the payment is completed.
Cancellation Policy: Hotel reservations made through this arrangement are non-cancellable. In case of special circumstances, please contact the IEEE RCAR 2026 Organizing Committee at ieeercar@163.com, or contact the travel agency sales manager, Mr. Zhang, at +86 188 1730 6697. Please indicate that you are with the IEEE RCAR team when contacting the travel agency.

Important Notes

  • The hotel environmental levy is not included. Current reference: SCR 100 per person per night for large hotels; children under 12 are exempt.
  • Rates are listed per room per night. The double-occupancy rate is based on two adults sharing one room; the single-occupancy rate is based on one adult occupying one room. Breakfast is included unless otherwise stated.
Seychelles Travel Authorisation: All travellers are required to apply for a Travel Authorisation before travelling to Seychelles. Participants should complete the online procedure through Seychelles Electronic Border System or the Seychelles E-Border app before departure.
For accommodation-related questions, please contact the IEEE RCAR 2026 Organizing Committee at ieeercar@163.com.
