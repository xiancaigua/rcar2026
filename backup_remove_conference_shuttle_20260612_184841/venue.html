Venue

IEEE RCAR 2026 will be held at Savoy Seychelles Resort & Spa, a 5-star beachfront resort located in Beau Vallon, Mahé. The resort provides conference, meeting, accommodation, dining, and leisure facilities suitable for international academic events.

Detailed room allocation, session layout, signage, and on-site registration arrangements will be announced after final confirmation by the organizing committee.

Airport Transportation

The conference hotel and venue is Savoy Seychelles Resort & Spa, located in Beau Vallon, Mahé. Most international participants will arrive at Seychelles International Airport (SEZ). The transfer from the airport to Savoy Seychelles Resort & Spa is approximately 45 minutes by car, depending on traffic and local road conditions.

Taxi Participants may take a taxi from the airport arrival area to Savoy Seychelles Resort & Spa. This is usually the most direct option for individual travelers with luggage.
Hotel-arranged Transfer Participants may contact the hotel or booking channel in advance to ask whether airport transfer service can be arranged. Availability and price depend on the hotel policy.
Pre-booked Private Transfer Private transfer is recommended for groups, late arrivals, or participants carrying large luggage. Please confirm pickup time, meeting point, and fare before arrival.

MICE Venue Highlights

  • Amirantes Ballroom is a grand, column-free hall with natural daylight.
  • The ballroom can be divided into three independent sections: Marie Louise, Desroches, and Alphonse.
  • Boardrooms I & II are suitable for executive meetings, VIP consultations, and breakout sessions.
  • The Lounge / Library / Sun Sail Zone can support small private meetings, interviews, press briefings, and creative setups.
  • Outdoor spaces include beachfront, pool deck, and garden areas.
  • Available meeting support includes AV systems, WiFi, projectors, microphones, and screens.
  • The resort provides MICE planning and event support services.
  • Final technical setup will be confirmed according to the official conference program.
