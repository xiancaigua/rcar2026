Workshop: Embodied Perception and Control of Aerial Unmanned Systems

Organizer: Hang Zhong ( Hunan University ) and Hean Hua ( Hunan University )

The rapid evolution of aerial unmanned systems—from micro drones to large-scale UAVs—demands a fundamental shift from traditional, modular approaches towards tightly integrated, embodied intelligence. This workshop explores the frontier of Embodied Perception and Control, where sensing, computation, and actuation are co-designed as a unified, adaptive system. We focus on how aerial platforms can leverage their physical structure, dynamics, and interaction with the environment to achieve unprecedented levels of autonomy, robustness, and efficiency. We now warmly invite you to submit your latest research and join us in shaping the next frontier of intelligent aerial robotics.

Key topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Embodied Sensing and Perception: Utilizing platform morphology, motion, and physical interaction for active and efficient perception.
  • Motion Planning and Navigation: Generating dynamically feasible, perception-aware trajectories in complex and uncertain environments.
  • Embodied Control: Agile and adaptive control strategies that are deeply integrated with real-time perceptual feedback and physical dynamics.
  • Autonomous Aerial Manipulation & Interaction: Control and planning for physical interaction, grasping, and manipulation tasks using aerial systems.
  • Multi-Robot Embodied Systems: Coordination, perception, and task allocation in teams of embodied aerial robots.
  • Field Deployments and Applications: Case studies in inspection, logistics, precision agriculture, search & rescue, and environmental monitoring.
