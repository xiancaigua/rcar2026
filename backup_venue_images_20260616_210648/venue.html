Venue

IEEE RCAR 2026 will be held at Savoy Seychelles Resort & Spa, Beau Vallon, Mahé, Seychelles. The conference venue room is AMIRANTES BALLROOM.

Important Entry Requirement: Seychelles Travel Authorisation

Although no visa is generally required for visitors to Seychelles, all travellers are required to apply for a Travel Authorisation (TA) before travelling to Seychelles. Participants may apply online through the Seychelles Electronic Border System or the Seychelles E-Border app before departure.
