Travel Information

Participants are encouraged to arrange international flights, accommodation, and local transportation early. Seychelles is a popular travel destination, and flight and hotel availability may be limited during the conference period. Detailed conference-specific transportation arrangements will be updated after confirmation by the organizing committee.

Arrival by Air

Most international participants will arrive in Seychelles through Seychelles International Airport (SEZ), located on Mahé Island. Participants should check flight availability and transit requirements carefully before booking, as most international trips to Seychelles may require transit through major international hubs.

International gateway: Seychelles International Airport (SEZ), Mahé Island

  • Participants should book flights to Seychelles International Airport (SEZ).
  • Please check airline schedules, transit visa requirements, baggage policies, and minimum connection times before booking.
  • Recommended flight routes and airline options will be updated later if confirmed by the organizing committee.
  • Participants are advised to arrive at least one day before their scheduled presentation or registration activity.
  • Possible transit hubs may include major international hubs in the Middle East, Africa, Europe, or Asia, depending on the participant's departure city and airline schedule. Participants should verify current routes directly with airlines or travel agencies.

Entry Requirements

Seychelles is generally a visa-free destination for visitors, but all travelers are required to complete a Travel Authorization before departure. Participants should check the latest official entry requirements before traveling.

Before departure, participants should prepare:

  • Valid passport
  • Return or onward flight ticket
  • Accommodation confirmation
  • Travel Authorization
  • Travel insurance, if required by airline or personal travel policy
  • Conference invitation letter, if needed

Registered participants who require an invitation letter may contact the conference secretariat at ieee.rcar@gmail.com. Please include your paper ID, paper title, full name, affiliation, passport name if needed, and registration confirmation.

Entry and transit requirements may change. Participants are responsible for checking the latest official requirements before departure.

Inter-island Transportation

Some participants may plan to visit other islands before or after the conference. Inter-island transportation in Seychelles may include domestic flights and ferry services between islands such as Mahé, Praslin, and La Digue.

  • Inter-island trips are optional and are not included in the conference registration fee unless otherwise specified.
  • Participants should check ferry or domestic flight schedules before planning island visits.
  • Weather and sea conditions may affect ferry schedules.
  • Participants should allow enough time when connecting between ferry, domestic flights, and international flights.

Useful Travel Tips

  • Book flights and hotels early.
  • Check passport validity and entry requirements before departure.
  • Complete Travel Authorization before departure.
  • Keep accommodation confirmation and return/onward flight information available for entry procedures.
  • Confirm airport transfer before arrival, especially for late-night flights.
  • Check local transportation options before planning independent travel.
  • Allow extra time for airport transfers, ferry connections, and inter-island travel.
  • Keep the conference contact email available: ieee.rcar@gmail.com
