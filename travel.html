Travel Information

Travel to Seychelles

Seychelles International Airport is the main international gateway for participants traveling to the conference. Detailed airport transfer and local transportation information will be announced later.

Entry Requirements

Participants should check the latest Seychelles entry requirements before traveling. Conference invitation letters may be provided to registered participants upon request.

Invitation Letter

Registered participants who require an invitation letter may contact the conference secretariat at ieee.rcar@gmail.com. Please include your paper ID, paper title, full name, affiliation, passport name if needed, and registration confirmation.
