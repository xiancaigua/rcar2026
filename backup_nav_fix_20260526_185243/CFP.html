Call for Papers

The 2026 IEEE International Conference on Real-time Computing and Robotics (IEEE RCAR 2026) will be held in Seychelles from August 16 to August 22, 2026. The conference welcomes high-quality submissions on robotics, real-time computing, intelligent systems, perception, control, and related applications.

Contributed Papers

Original papers are solicited in all related areas of real-time computing and robotics. Accepted and presented full papers are expected to be submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore, subject to IEEE requirements.

Tutorials and Workshops

Proposals for tutorials and workshops addressing emerging topics in real-time computing and robotics are invited.

Journal Publications

Expanded versions of selected accepted and presented papers may be invited for publication in related journals.

Important Dates

Full Paper Submission Deadline: April 20, 2026

Acceptance Notification: May 20, 2026

Final Paper Submission Deadline: June 20, 2026

Advance Registration Deadline: June 20, 2026
